P1-B2 request/event audit
=========================
Status: PASS against the CI harness APK.
The validation receiver was exercised on-device after installing
com.retroarch.aarch64.p1b2. Init, snapshot, reset, pause, resume, slot change,
save, load, screenshot, RA menu, close content, and repeated/burst submissions
returned OK where content state permitted. Request IDs 1 through 20 were
monotonic; each accepted request had a matching completion event. The expected
no-content RA menu toggle returned INVALID_STATE and did not crash.

The public snapshot reports operation_busy=NOT_EXPOSED_PUBLIC_API; therefore
busy was checked indirectly through rapid queue submissions and final recovery,
not by claiming an unavailable field.
